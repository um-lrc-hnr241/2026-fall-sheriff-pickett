Confessions of St. Augustine and Westworld

We explore connections between our team reading, Confessions by St. Augustine, and the television series Westworld.

Meet our team

About the project

This site shares the work we did in class and the connections our group made between a work of spiritual reflection and a story about consciousness, memory, and identity.

As we consider Augustine’s reflections alongside the world of Westworld, we invite readers to think about how people understand themselves and the experiences that shape them.

Questions we’re exploring

What makes a person who they are? How do memory and self-reflection shape our understanding of ourselves?

These questions guide our comparison of Confessions and Westworld.
